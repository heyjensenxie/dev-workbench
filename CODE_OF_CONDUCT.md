# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and prioritize the safety of contributors and users. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers, either through the repository's
[private security advisory form](https://github.com/heyjensenxie/dev-workbench/security/advisories/new)
(marked as a conduct report, not a vulnerability) or by contacting the maintainer
[@heyjensenxie](https://github.com/heyjensenxie) directly. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers recuse themselves from any report that concerns them.

This is a small project without a dedicated conduct committee; the practical
commitment is that a report is read, acknowledged, and acted on rather than
silently dropped.
